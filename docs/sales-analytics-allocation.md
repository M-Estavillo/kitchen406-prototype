# Sales Analytics and preparing owners

Preparing owners are recorded per order item (per cake or product within a subscription delivery). Owner A receives 100%, Owner B receives 100%, or both receive a configurable whole-number split. These are prototype owner labels, independent of the account clicking fulfillment actions. Staff are never earnings recipients.

Assign owners from owner order details or staff production details, or within a status confirmation. Subscription batch confirmations provide individual product allocations for each fulfillment. Apply-to-all copies a selection across the products of that fulfillment. Each delivery can have different owners. Allocation locks when courier booking is active or the order is completed. Failed/cancelled bookings allow corrections. Existing records are not assigned automatically.

Sales Analytics defaults to fulfilled product sales, with owner, product, order type, and date filters. The Unallocated filter exposes completed historical work without attribution. Recorded payments remains a separate reporting basis; prepaid parent purchases are counted only there. Fulfilled reporting uses completed delivery dates and the original prepaid product consideration, weighted by product selling prices, excluding delivery fees. Rounding preserves the paid product total across deliveries and owner shares. Ingredient, packaging and utility costs use current estimates, so the profit metric is explicitly an estimate. Allocated units are proportional to the earnings share.

This is an in-memory prototype following the existing application state model. Allocation is attached to the operational order; no backend persistence or real owner-account provisioning is added. Prototype reload/reset behavior follows the existing order stores. Allocation edits from details are recorded in status history with the acting account, and normal fulfillment actions retain their existing history.
