

Phase 1 — Brand Foundation, Storefront & Authentication
Global visual language, responsive customer navigation, product catalog, product variants, availability, customer reviews, sign-in, registration, email verification, password reset, and authentication states.
Phase 2 — Cart, Checkout, Payment & Orders
Cart management, fulfillment date, pickup/delivery, address selection, delivery fee, totals, capacity/stock hold explanation, QR Ph payment flow, order history/detail, status timeline, cancellation/retry states, and post-purchase reviews.
Phase 3 — Customer Subscriptions
Four-week prepaid subscription enrollment, product/quantity/schedule selection, four-delivery preview, pricing breakdown, active subscription management, delivery-cycle status, and one-time deferment to next day/next week.
Phase 4 — Custom Cakes & Customer Account
Cake builder/request flow, options/add-ons, reference image, preliminary estimate, delivery configuration, quotation history and acceptance, saved addresses, profile, email/password changes, and notification history.
Phase 5 — Staff Operations Workspace
Staff dashboard, production queue, order preparation, inventory stock, stock movements, recipes without financial information, production calendar, courier status visibility, notifications, and staff account settings. Staff must never see customer identity/contact/address or owner financial data.
Phase 6 — Owner Operations & Configuration
Owner dashboard, full order management, payment-resolution cases, courier booking, inventory/costing, recipes, custom-cake quotation workflow, subscription commitments, capacity calendar, blocked dates, schedules, catalog/product management, pricing, ingredients, cake options/add-ons, and seasonal occasions.
Phase 7 — Owner Administration, Moderation & Business Tools
Account/staff management, customer-review moderation, marketing draft review/edit/regenerate/approve flows, sales and estimated-margin reporting, operational notifications/outbox, and the prototype/testing lab.
